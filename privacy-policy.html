Welluma Health Inc. and its affiliates (collectively, "Welluma Health", "us", "we", or "our") are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your Personal Information when you use the Welluma Health mobile application, our website at wellumahealth.com, and related services (collectively, the "Services"). By using the Services, you agree to the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information in accordance with this Policy.

🇨🇦 Canadian Company — Data Stored in Canada

1. Definition of Personal Information

In this Policy, "Personal Information" means any information about an identifiable individual.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our Services, we may collect or receive Personal Information about you to provide our services and operate our business.

Information you provide directly

You may provide your Personal Information to us when you use our Services, including when you create an account, make a purchase, contact us, or otherwise access our Services. This information may include:

  • Account information: name and email address when you create an account
  • Audio recordings of medical appointments you choose to record using the App
  • Text transcripts automatically generated from your audio recordings
  • Messages you send us through the contact form or by email
  • Health provider information, if you choose to upload or save such information to your account
  • Promotional code associated with your account, where applicable

Information collected automatically

We may also receive and store certain information about you and your device automatically when you access or use our Services, including:

  • Technical Information: browser and operating system details, IP address, unique device identifiers, and device type — used for diagnostic and statistical purposes
  • Site Usage Information: pages visited, time spent, and traffic patterns — used to improve the Services
  • Site Preferences: cookie-based preferences to make your use of the Site more productive

The App itself does not use cookies. The App may collect anonymous usage analytics (such as features used and session duration) to help us improve the Services. This data is not linked to your health information or recording content.

3. Use of Your Personal Information

We may use the Personal Information we collect or receive about you for the following purposes:

  • To provide the Services: processing audio recordings to generate transcripts and AI-powered summaries, managing your account and visit history
  • To maintain and improve the Services: analytics, auditing, and improving content and functionality. We do not use your Personal Information for advertising personalization.
  • To communicate with you: service updates, policy changes, and responding to support inquiries
  • For security purposes: combating spam, malware, and unauthorized access
  • For legal and regulatory compliance: fulfilling business obligations and managing risk as required by law
  • To enforce our Terms of Use and protect you, others, and our business

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website (wellumahealth.com) may use cookies to help us understand how visitors interact with the Site and to improve your browsing experience. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, though some Site features may not function fully as a result. We do not support Do Not Track (DNT) signals.

5. Sharing Your Information

Your Personal Information is not shared with third parties without your permission, except as described below.

Service Providers

We share information with third-party service providers solely to deliver our Services. These providers are obligated not to disclose or use your Personal Information for any other purpose.

Anthropic (AI Processing): When you request a visit summary, the text transcript of your appointment is transmitted securely to Anthropic, PBC (anthropic.com) solely for the purpose of generating your plain-language summary. Transcripts are not retained by Anthropic after processing. You will be asked for explicit consent before any transcript is sent. Anthropic's privacy policy is available at anthropic.com/privacy.

Supabase (Cloud Storage): Your account information and visit history are stored on servers operated by Supabase Inc., a SOC 2 Type II certified provider. Data is stored in Canada (ca-central-1 region) and is subject to strict confidentiality agreements.

RevenueCat (Subscription Management): Subscription status and purchase history are managed by RevenueCat Inc. RevenueCat receives your app user ID and subscription transaction data to verify and manage your subscription. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Apple / Google (Payment Processing): In-app purchases are processed by Apple Inc. (App Store) or Google LLC (Google Play). We do not receive or store your payment card details.

When Required by Law

We may disclose your information to law enforcement or other third parties when required to protect our legal rights, protect user safety, prevent fraud, or comply with legal process.

Corporate Transactions

In the event of a sale, transfer, merger, or similar transaction, your information may be transferred to one or more third parties as part of that transaction.

With Your Consent

We may also share or disclose your information with your explicit consent or at your direction.

6. Security of Personal Information

We have implemented reasonable organizational, technological, and physical security measures to safeguard your Personal Information. Personal information is encrypted at rest and in transit. Our cloud infrastructure provider (Supabase) operates within a SOC 2 Type II certified environment using industry-standard security controls.

No online activity is ever fully secure. While we strive to protect your Personal Information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. We urge you to take precautions to protect your Personal Information when using the Internet.

7. Retention and Destruction

We will retain your Personal Information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services. Audio recordings and transcripts can be deleted by you at any time through the App. When you delete your account, your Personal Information — including recordings, transcripts, and visit summaries — will be securely destroyed or anonymized within a reasonable timeframe. When Personal Information is no longer required, we will securely destroy or anonymize it.

8. Your Rights

  • Right to Know: You have the right to know the Personal Information we collect, use, and disclose about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may modify your Personal Information by contacting us. You can also request a copy of your Personal Information in a commonly used electronic format.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request that we delete your Personal Information. You can also delete your recordings, transcripts, and summaries directly through the App at any time.

To exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@wellumahealth.com. We will endeavour to respond to written requests within thirty (30) days.

9. Transfer of Personal Information

Welluma Health is based in Canada. All information collected through our Services is transferred to and maintained on servers located in Canada. Any information we obtain about you will be stored in accordance with Canadian privacy laws and regulations, including PHIPA. By using our Services, you consent to this collection, transfer, storage, and processing of information in Canada.

10. Third-Party Sites

11. Electronic Communications
