The Real Problem

Most patients don't leave without information.
They leave without understanding.

Whether it's a new diagnosis, medication changes, test results, or a follow-up plan, healthcare appointments can feel overwhelming. By the time you get home, it's easy to forget what was said or wonder what it all means. Welluma helps you revisit your appointments with clarity, understand medical information in plain language, and feel more confident about your next steps.

1

Record your visit

Open Welluma before your appointment. The app captures the conversation — no manual notes required.

Always obtain consent from all parties before recording, in accordance with the laws of your jurisdiction.

2

Get a plain-language summary

Welluma turns the conversation into a clear, organized summary of what was discussed, recommended, and next steps — within minutes.

3

Access trusted resources

Each summary links to curated, evidence-based health information so you can learn more, follow through on recommendations, and arrive better prepared for follow-ups.

More Than a Summary

A summary isn't the same as understanding.

Many healthcare systems can provide a visit summary. Welluma goes further — here's what that looks like in practice.

Understand Your Appointment

Revisit your visit in plain language, any time you need to — so nothing important gets lost between the clinic and home.

Know What It Means

Learn what medical terms, diagnoses, and treatment plans actually mean — in language you don't need a medical degree to follow.

Explore Trusted Information

Every summary links to curated, evidence-based resources related to your care — so you know exactly where to learn more.

Share Your Care

Share your visit with family or caregivers when you choose. Continuity of care shouldn't depend on anyone's memory.

Prepare for What's Next

Keep your appointment history organized in one place, so you can arrive at every follow-up informed and ready with the right questions.

Because understanding your health shouldn't require a medical degree.

Your Health, Beyond the Clinic

Your healthcare journey continues after you leave the clinic.

The appointment may be over, but questions often begin afterward. Welluma helps you revisit your appointments whenever you need to, so you can feel informed, prepared, and more confident in managing your health.

Who It's For

Made for patients. Valued by providers.

Whether you're managing your own health or supporting the people in your care, Welluma closes the gap between the clinic and everyday life.

For Patients

You deserve to fully understand your own health. Welluma makes sure you leave every appointment informed, not overwhelmed.

  • Capture everything your provider says — even when you're anxious or tired
  • Understand diagnoses, medications, and next steps in plain English
  • Share summaries with family members who help manage your care
  • Build a complete, searchable health history over time
  • Arrive at follow-ups prepared with the right questions

For Healthcare Providers

Your patients leave understanding what you said. That means better adherence, fewer repeat calls, and more meaningful appointments.

  • Patients show up more engaged and informed at follow-ups
  • Medication and care plan adherence improves with written summaries
  • Reduces the burden of follow-up phone calls and clarification questions
  • Supports patients with language barriers or health literacy challenges
  • Complements existing EMR workflows — no integration required

Privacy Comes First

Your health information is personal.

Welluma is designed with privacy and security in mind, giving you control over your information and who you choose to share it with.

End-to-end encrypted

Recordings and summaries are encrypted in transit and at rest. No one can read your data but you.

Never sold

Your health information is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train AI models.

Delete any time

You own your data completely. Delete individual appointments or your entire account at any time.

You control sharing

Summaries are private by default. You choose if, when, and with whom any summary is shared.

Why I Built Welluma

Patients should never feel powerless in a siloed healthcare system.

I built Welluma because patients deserve to have their health information connected, understandable, and within their control — no matter how many providers, clinics, or hospitals are involved in their care.

Welluma gives patients the knowledge and tools to take an active role in their health and future. It also helps families stay informed and better support the people they love, when the patient chooses to include them.

Connected care, not silos

Bring the patient's health story together so information can follow them across their healthcare journey.

Power belongs with the patient

Give patients the understanding and control they need to make informed decisions about their health and future.

Better support for loved ones

Help families understand what is happening and provide meaningful support — when the patient wants them involved.
